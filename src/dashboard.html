Scanning

Sends a scan request to every scannable device, so its scan response arrives too — that is often where the device name is. Off = passive: the scanner never transmits and is invisible to the devices around it, but sees fewer names.
Picks up BLE 5 long-range advertisers, which few devices use. The radio then splits its time between the 1M and Coded PHYs, so it catches fewer packets from ordinary devices.

Scanning is continuous with no duplicate filter: every advertisement and scan response is received, legacy and BLE 5 extended. Wi-Fi shares the same radio, so while Wi-Fi is on the scanner gets less than 100 % of the air time. Reports the radio controller itself discards under heavy load are not counted here.

Sensor & scale

Range & filter apply immediately — not while recording. With Remember off (default), the device always boots at ±2 g.
When on, a recording that clips (saturates full scale) for several seconds is automatically continued in a new part at a larger range — headless, no dashboard needed. Escalates up only, up to ±16 g.

Sensor self-test

IIS3DWB electrostatic self-test — checks each axis against the datasheet's 800-3200 mg range. Keep the device still; not during a recording.

Normally the device joins the strongest AP of its network. Pin it to one specific AP so several BTScanner devices share the same access point — needed for time-aligned (TSF) recordings. If that AP is out of range it falls back to a normal connect, so this never strands the device.
